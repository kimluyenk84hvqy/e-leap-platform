What makes a sport exciting?
LESSON AIMS

Aims of the Lesson

By the end of the lesson, learners will be able to:

01

Knowledge

  • Identify vocabulary for wrestling, Olympic sports, clubs and membership.
  • Distinguish uses of like and key expressions for interests, plans and recommendations.
02

Skills

  • Read for main ideas, specific information and implied feelings; listen for gist and detail.
  • Discuss sports and clubs, explain preferences and recommend activities clearly.
03

Attitude

  • Respect different opinions about sports and cultural traditions.
  • Collaborate actively and encourage others during pair and group work.
