The Idiotarod
Read quietly for 60 seconds. Write only five keywords. Do not show this card to Partner B.
The Idiotarod is an annual race in twenty different US cities. Each team must have five people and a shopping cart. One person usually rides in the cart and four people pull it. Teams can decorate the shopping cart but they can’t change the wheels. All the teams have to start and finish at the same place but they don’t have to run on the same roads. The members of each team must cross the finish line together and they mustn’t finish without the cart.

The Mud Bowl Championship
Read quietly for 60 seconds. Write only five keywords. Do not show this card to Partner A.
Mud Bowl football is similar to normal American football, so players can pick up the ball and run with it. There are also two teams, but the match is shorter. The winner is the team with the most goals at the end of sixty minutes. The really big difference is that the players have to play in a field with half a metre of mud.
