LESSON AIMS

Aims of the Lesson

By the end of the lesson, learners will be able to:

01

Knowledge

  • Identify and classify key vocabulary for competitions and sportspeople.
  • Distinguish language for obligation, permission, prohibition and lack of obligation.
02

Skills

  • Analyze key information and opinions in the reading and listening texts.
  • Apply target language to explain rules, justify choices, retell ideas and use common -ing patterns.
03

Attitude

  • Evaluate different views about winning and competitive sport respectfully.
  • Collaborate actively by listening, responding and sharing responsibility in pair and group work.

1 · DecideCorrect or Needs a change?

B

In swimming, competitors don’t have to enter the water.

C

In tennis, players don’t have to hit the ball over the net.

Possible corrections: A goalkeeper can pick up the ball only in the penalty area. · Swimmers have to enter the water. · Tennis players have to hit the ball over the net.
