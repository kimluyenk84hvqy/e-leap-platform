Dear Peter,

I’m glad to hear from you and I’m very happy that you’re visiting in September with your family. Sorry I haven’t written for so long — I’ve been busy studying for my exams.

Anyway, I’m writing to answer your questions. The weather is still fairly warm, so you could spend some time at the beach. Your parents might also enjoy the old town and the music festival.

As for clothes, light summer clothes will be fine, but bring a sweater or jacket for the evenings. Don’t forget your swimming costume!

Must dash now! Please send my wishes to your parents and drop me a line when you have time.

Love,

ACTIVITY 3 · DIAGNOSE

3. Why would this answer lose marks?

Click the problems you notice. Think about content, organisation, language, style and editing.

Dear Mary,

I am writing to inform you that your proposed visit will be enjoyable. There are many things. You can visit the lake. You can visit a museum. You can eat food.

Clothes are important. Bring clothes. The weather is weather. I wait your answer.

Yours faithfully,

REVISION

Repair the weak paragraph

Rewrite the paragraph so that it sounds friendly, answers the task and develops at least one idea.

Weak version

There are many things. You can visit the lake. You can visit a museum. You can eat food.

Try: suggestion + reason + detail.
