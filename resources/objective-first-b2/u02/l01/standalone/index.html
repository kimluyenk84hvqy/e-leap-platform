    HOMEWORK

    Review and extend

    1. Review the four game reviews and note two useful collocations.
    2. Write five correct -ly adverbs from the lesson and their related adjectives.
    3. Choose one advantage and one disadvantage of computer games and develop each with Why? + Result/Example.

    Suggested answers

    1. Useful collocations: solve puzzles; spend time; reach a goal; take control.
    2. Adverbs + adjectives: easily → easy; endlessly → endless; graphically → graphic; remarkably → remarkable; truly → true.
    3. Sample development:
      Advantage – Social connection: Online games let players communicate and cooperate with other people. This can help them practise teamwork and make friends.
      Disadvantage – Addiction: Some players spend excessive amounts of time gaming. As a result, they may have less time for study and face-to-face interaction.
