GRAMMAR FOCUS

Stative or Action?

Emotions: like, love, dislike, hate, prefer, want

Mind/opinions: understand, remember, believe, know, seem, agree, disagree

Senses: smell, taste, feel, hear/sound, see

States: be, have

Action verbs: drive, run, play, teach, take a photo

BOOK VOCABULARY BOX

Word Formation

TRUE → truth · true · truly

Negative prefixes: happy → unhappy; regular → irregular; possible → impossible.

Suffixes can change word class: happy → happiness; friend → friendly; quick → quickly.

Many nouns form related adjectives with -y: nerd → nerdy.
