# U6 Reading Jigsaw — acceptance-focused upgrade

## Source-bound constraints
Teacher supplied Objective PET B1, Unit 6 p.41, Reading “My first day”: original A/B/C text and three printed photographs. Reading 1 matches paragraphs to photos; Reading 2 asks for names and estimated start years. Calendar years are estimates, not unique auto-graded answers. The source is scanned; source TXT and images remain local/teacher-held and are not uploaded to GitHub.

## Method selection
For a home group of three, teacher assigns **A, B, C** individually. Expert groups compare the SAME assigned account; home groups each teach their account, then complete **both original book tasks**, then give evidence and why for each match and reasoned year estimates. Do not turn into serial whole-class A→B→C reading.

## Limits (fail closed)
Preview Studio adapter carries assignment labels but learner-level isolation and authenticated group assignment are NOT implemented. Until then, all three source blocks may be accessible in the teacher draft; do not claim enforced jigsaw access. No original photos embedded in public repo. No verified answer key, automatic grading, student submission, responses persistence or publication.

## M1/M3 acceptance
M1 requires source text/photograph alignment checked by a teacher, location data, image attachment, and a verified-answer record where objective grading applies.
M3 requires real shared-runtime student role behavior, assignment isolation, teacher review, per-task submit to Responses and browser E2E. This commit only improves review-stage pedagogical contract.
